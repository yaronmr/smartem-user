How to use this page Key sections saved: …

The automatic plan's outlines on every section. Correct the outlines on a few key sections and save each one; every other section is filled in from them at once (long-dashed outlines), and the plan is built from your key sections later. Nothing here touches a plan file or the microscope.

  1. Pick a section: click one under Correct these next (right), or move with the slider, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel. Ctrl + wheel zooms; drag to move the picture.
  2. Fix its outlines. If the outlines have the right shape but sit beside the tissue, press Move outlines and drag them into place. If the shape is wrong, press Brush … and paint the area, or press Edit points and drag the points. An outline you don't touch is saved as it is shown.
  3. Press Save as key section (or Ctrl + S). Every section between your key sections follows at once. Changes you have not saved exist only in this browser tab; the page lists them under Not saved yet.

Keys: ← → change the section; H (hold) hides the outlines; M turns Move on and off; Ctrl + Z undoes; hold Space and drag to move the picture in any tool.

  1. The coloured area is the current outline. With + Add selected, drag on the image to colour tissue the outline misses.
  2. Click − Remove (or hold Shift, or drag with the right mouse button) and drag over anything the outline should not include.
  3. Press Done: make outline. The coloured area becomes the new outline (holes are filled, stray specks dropped).
  4. Press Save as key section.
