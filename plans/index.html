The automatic plan's outlines on every section. Draw or correct the outline on a few key sections and save each one; every other section is filled in from them at once (long-dashed outlines), and the plan is built from your key sections later. Nothing here touches a plan file or the microscope.

Connecting to saved key sections…
  1. Find a section (slider, arrow keys or mouse wheel; Ctrl + wheel zooms; drag to pan).
  2. Press Edit outlines and node. Drag a point; drag the small circle on an edge, or click the edge, to add a point; right-click or Alt-click a point to remove it; Ctrl+Z undoes. Or press Draw … anew and click (or trace) a whole new outline.
  3. Press Save as key section. Scroll through: the other sections follow at once. Fix the worst remaining one, save it, repeat.

Pick the outline to fix; you then colour the tissue with a brush and the page draws the outline.
Brushing
  1. The coloured area is the current outline. With + Add selected, drag on the image to colour tissue the outline misses.
  2. Click − Remove (or hold Shift, or drag with the right mouse button) and drag over anything the outline should not include.
  3. Press Done: make outline. The coloured area becomes the new outline (holes are filled, stray specks dropped).
  4. Press Save as key section.
Size: slider, or the [ and ] keys. Mouse wheel zooms; hold Space and drag to move the picture; Ctrl+Z undoes a stroke; Esc cancels.
