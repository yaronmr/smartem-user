How to use Plan Desk

Plan Desk shows the microscope's planned outlines on every section of a wafer. You correct the outlines on a few sections, called key sections, and every other section is filled in from them. The imaging plan is built later from your key sections. Nothing you do here moves the microscope.

1. Get access (once)

  1. On the Plan Desk page, choose Register. Enter your name, your email and a password (at least 8 characters).
  2. Open the confirmation email and click its link. You come back to Plan Desk, which says Waiting for approval.
  3. The person who invited you gives you access. Then press Check again or reload the page.

Next time, just Sign in. Forgot your password? Choose Forgot password and follow the emailed link.

2. What you see

  • Choose a wafer (for example W15 plan). One section is shown at a time.
  • Green outline: the Subumbrella. Red outline: the NR.
  • Long-dashed outlines were filled in from the key sections around this one. Solid outlines are a key section or your edit in progress (before any key section exists, the automatic plan is shown solid). A faint dotted outline shows the original automatic plan underneath.
  • The cyan dot is a focus point; you don't need to move it.
  • Move between sections with the slider, the ← → keys or the mouse wheel. Ctrl + wheel zooms; drag the picture to move it.

3. Correct a section

  1. Choose where. In the right-hand box, Correct these next lists the sections that most need fixing, worst first. Click one to go there.
  2. Fix an outline, the easy way: the brush. Press Brush Subumbrella or Brush NR. The coloured area is the current outline.
    • With + Add selected, drag over tissue the outline misses.
    • Press − Remove (or hold Shift, or drag with the right mouse button) and drag over anything it should not include.
    • Change the brush size with the slider or the [ ] keys. Ctrl+Z undoes a stroke.
    • Press Done: make outline. The coloured area becomes the new outline.
  3. Or move points. Press Edit outlines and node. Drag a point; click an edge to add a point; right-click a point to remove it.
  4. Fix both outlines if both are off. An outline you don't touch is saved exactly as it is shown.
  5. Press Save as key section. The sections between your key sections update at once.

4. Check and repeat

  • Scroll through the sections. Wherever a long-dashed outline drifts off the tissue, correct that section and save it too.
  • Your key sections (right) lists every key section. For each outline it says drawn (someone redrew it) or as shown (saved without redrawing). Ticks under the section slider mark where they are.
  • About 8–10 well-spread key sections are usually enough. The list Correct these next is refreshed after new key sections are saved.

Questions

I made a mistake before saving.
Press Ctrl+Z to undo, or go to another section without saving.
I saved a section I shouldn't have.
Go to it and press Back to the plan. That removes its key section.
Someone else is working on the same wafer.
Everyone sees the same key sections, and the page refreshes them about every 20 seconds. Saving a section again replaces the earlier version. Every save is kept on record.
The picture doesn't load.
Reload the page. If it still fails, tell the person who invited you.
Does anything I do change the microscope?
No. The imaging plan is built from the saved key sections later, and checked before it is used.
