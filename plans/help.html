How to use Plan Desk

Plan Desk shows the microscope's planned outlines on every section of a wafer. You correct the outlines on a few sections, called key sections, and every other section is filled in from them. The imaging plan is built later from your key sections. Nothing you do here moves the microscope.

1. Get access (once)

  1. On the Plan Desk page, choose Register. Enter your name, your email and a password (at least 8 characters).
  2. Open the confirmation email and click its link. You come back to Plan Desk, which says Waiting for approval.
  3. Tell the person who invited you that you have registered: Plan Desk does not email them. Once they give you access, the page opens by itself within half a minute (or press Check again).

Next time, just Sign in. Forgot your password? Choose Forgot password and follow the emailed link.

2. What you see

  • Choose a wafer (for example W15 plan). One section is shown at a time.
  • Green outline: the Subumbrella. Red outline: the NR.
  • Long-dashed outlines were filled in from the key sections around this one. Solid outlines are a key section or your edit in progress (before any key section exists, the automatic plan is shown solid). A faint dotted outline shows the original automatic plan underneath.
  • The cyan dot is a focus point; you don't need to move it.
  • Move between sections with the slider, the ← → keys or the mouse wheel (Page Up / Page Down jump ten sections, Home / End go to the first and last). Ctrl + wheel zooms; drag the picture to move it. The page reopens on the section you were last on.
  • Hold H to hide the outlines and see the tissue under them.
  • A section you have changed and not saved yet is marked not saved on the picture, with a tick under the slider, and is listed under Not saved yet on the right.

3. Correct a section

  1. Choose where. In the right-hand box, Correct these next lists the sections that most need fixing, worst first. Click one to go there.
  2. Right shape, wrong place: move it. Press Move outlines (or M) and drag on the picture; the outlines follow the mouse.
    • Hold Alt and drag to turn them, or press Turn left / Turn right (1° each).
    • For small steps use the arrow buttons or Shift + arrow keys; choose the step (0.5 to 20 µm) next to them.
    • Move chooses all outlines or only one. The focus node moves with them unless you untick it.
    • The page says how far the section has been moved. Move stays on while you go through the sections; Done moving or Esc turns it off.
  3. Wrong shape: the brush. Press Brush Subumbrella or Brush NR. The coloured area is the current outline.
    • With + Add selected, drag over tissue the outline misses.
    • Press − Remove (or hold Shift, or drag with the right mouse button) and drag over anything it should not include.
    • Change the brush size with the slider or the [ ] keys. Ctrl+Z undoes a stroke.
    • Press Done: make outline. The coloured area becomes the new outline.
  4. Or move single points. Press Edit points. Drag a point; click an edge to add a point; right-click a point to remove it. The focus node can be dragged here too.
  5. Fix both outlines if both are off. An outline you don't touch is saved exactly as it is shown.
  6. Press Save as key section (or Ctrl+S). The page answers saved as a key section, or says plainly that it was not saved and why. The sections between your key sections update at once, and Next to correct takes you to the next suggested section.
  7. You can correct several sections before saving. They are listed under Not saved yet; Save all of them saves them together. Unsaved changes exist only in your browser tab: the page warns you before you close it or sign out.

4. Check and repeat

  • Scroll through the sections. Wherever a long-dashed outline drifts off the tissue, correct that section and save it too.
  • Your key sections (right) lists every key section. For each outline it says drawn (someone redrew it) or as shown (saved without redrawing). Ticks under the section slider mark where they are.
  • About 8–10 well-spread key sections are usually enough. The list Correct these next is updated from time to time as key sections are added; between updates, trust your own eye.

Questions

I made a mistake before saving.
Press Undo (Ctrl+Z) to go back one step, or Discard my changes to drop everything you changed on this section since its last save. If you discard by mistake, Put my changes back appears right after.
I saved a section I shouldn't have.
Go to it and press Remove key section. The page asks first, because this changes the outlines for everyone. Put the key section back appears right after, in case it was a mistake.
Someone else is working on the same wafer.
Everyone sees the same key sections, and the page refreshes them about every 20 seconds. Each key section shows who saved it and when. If someone saves a section while you are changing the same one, the page tells you when you save and lets you choose: keep yours, or take theirs. Every save is kept on record.
The picture doesn't load.
The pictures come from a Harvard server that some browsers refuse; this has been seen with Firefox. Open Plan Desk in Chrome or Edge. If the picture is still missing, press Try again on the message, and if that fails tell the person who invited you. Do not correct outlines without the picture.
The buttons are greyed out and the page says "View only".
Your account can look at the plans but not change them. Ask the person who invited you for editing access.
The page asks me to sign in again while I am working.
Sign-ins end after a while. Enter your password in the box; your unsaved changes stay on the page. Then press Save again.
Does anything I do change the microscope?
No. The imaging plan is built from the saved key sections later, and checked before it is used.
