Match Desk

Match the same spot on two neighbouring image sections. Sign in to continue. New accounts are approved by a referee before they can see or add anything.

How this works
  1. Register with your email, confirm it from the email you get, and wait for a referee to approve your account. Until then you can look but not save.
  2. Requests list the section pair and the area that needs matches, with a target number of pairs. Spread them over every cube outline.
  3. Click a spot on one section, then the same tissue on the other. Drag a marker to move it, Esc cancels a half-made pair.
  4. Each pair is checked as you add it: against the automatic correction once there is one (more than 15 px off), otherwise against the pairs around it (more than 27 px off). A pair that disagrees is marked flagged: look again and move it or delete it. Pairs at the edge of the clicked area aren't judged until there are pairs around them.
  5. Yellow dotted boxes are areas that need a minimum number of pairs. Orange diamonds are spots the automatic matching was least sure of: put one pair near each (it turns green).
  6. Check other people's pairs: Confirm if it is the same tissue, Dispute if not. You can only move or delete your own pairs.
  7. Some pairs are set aside at random to test the correction, so the fit is scored on points it never saw. You don't need to do anything about this.
  8. Coordinates are full-resolution stack pixels (x, y, z).
